## Steps

1. Check the barred list further down. Melee's app may load even where its terms bar you.
2. Go to melee.markets and press Enter beta.
3. Log in. The app offers "Continue with email or social", Google or a Solana wallet, and says: "No crypto wallet needed. We'll set one up for you."
4. Add a code if you have one, through its link or Claim a code on your profile. The site says "Access drops in waves", with "Codes released regularly on Twitter and Discord." We saw none in Melee's X posts from 26 to 31 October, and we couldn't test whether a code is still required.
5. Set a username and take the tutorial. The welcome case "Opens once your profile and the tutorial are done."
6. Open the daily case and trade each day to keep a streak. If you want to create markets, apply through the form in Melee's Phase 3 post.
