## Steps

1. Use only squeekchain.com and docs.squeekchain.com, both linked from @squeekchain .
2. Link your X account. A whitelist place needs an X account at least one year old.
3. Link a Discord account that is at least 365 days old for the Discord badge.
4. If you held a listed NFT at its snapshot, link that wallet. Buying one now won't count, because the snapshots have passed.
5. Earn more Aura by inviting friends, playing Exsqueektion while the hamster is on stream, or building a project.
6. Check your rank on the Genesis leaderboard and watch @squeekchain for mint details.
