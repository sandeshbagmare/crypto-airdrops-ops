## Steps

1. Check you can enter. You can't if you live in the EEA or another restricted region, or if you're a market maker, an institution or Pro 3 or above. Warning Don't use a VPN. Bybit's restricted-countries page says a false location can lead to "termination of any Account immediately and liquidating any open positions".
2. Complete identity checks. "Users must complete Identity Verification Lv. 1 to be eligible for WSOT." Only Unified Trading Accounts (UTA) count.
3. Register on the WSOT 2026 event page . Type bybit.com yourself and avoid links from strangers. "Register each eligible account separately. You may register up to 1 UTA Main Account and 6 UTA Subaccounts."
4. Choose how to compete. "Traders can join a squad and compete solo at the same time, or compete solo only." To join a squad, Bybit's announcement gives 19 November at 10:00 UTC. Its Learn guide says members can't leave or switch after 13 November at 09:30 UTC, so join before then to be safe.
5. Do the Preheat Quest tasks on your main account.
6. For leaderboards, fund the account: "To activate a PnL or PnL% leaderboard entry, you need to maintain at least 100 USDT in equity for five minutes."
7. Trade in your track's window and meet its volume minimum. Rewards are paid within 30 days after the end.
