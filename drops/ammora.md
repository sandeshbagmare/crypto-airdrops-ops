## Steps

1. Add GIWA Sepolia to a browser wallet such as MetaMask. GIWA's docs give chain ID 91342 and the RPC address https://sepolia-rpc.giwa.io.
2. Get GIWA Sepolia test ETH from one of the faucets linked on the waitlist page.
3. Open the waitlist join page , connect your wallet, sign the message and enter an email address. Signing sends no transaction.
4. Choose your X account carefully, then do the one-time X tasks. The privacy notice says "A different X account may replace the linked account once", so you get one change.
5. Each day, use Supply to deposit at least 0.001 test ETH. Once a week, from Monday 00:00 UTC, publish the prepared post on X. Warning Posting the same promotional text again and again can get an X account restricted. Ammora cut the post to once a week after participants reported restrictions and suspensions.
6. Check the Boost page for the daily roulette, bridge and swap activities. Season 2 of the Boost event ended on 16 October 2026, and no new season had been announced by 18 October.
7. Check your rank on the leaderboard and follow the Telegram channel for announcements.
