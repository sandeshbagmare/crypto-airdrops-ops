## Steps

1. Read the program terms . They exclude the US, the UK, Canada and 15 other countries.
2. Set up Robinhood Wallet for the 2x rate, or open robinhoodchain.lighter.xyz with another wallet for 1x.
3. Get USDG on Robinhood Chain and deposit it as margin.
4. Trade perpetual futures. Part of your points balance updates as you trade, and the rest arrives in the Friday drop.

## Costs

You need USDG for margin. Funding settles every hour between long and short traders, and a liquidation carries a fee of up to 1%, according to Robinhood's support page. The Block reported that these perpetuals would carry no fees for their first 90 days. We couldn't confirm the current trading fee, so check it in the app. Warning Leverage is the main cost. Robinhood's own example shows a 20% move 

## Risks

Points and trading are both restricted in Belarus, Canada, China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Singapore, South Sudan, Sudan, Switzerland, Syria, Ukraine, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, the United States and Venezuela. Lighter can remove or reverse points at any time, even after the program ends. Its terms rule out wash trading, multiple accounts, referral abuse and bot
