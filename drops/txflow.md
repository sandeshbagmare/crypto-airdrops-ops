## Steps

1. Check that you're allowed. The terms exclude the United States, China, Singapore, Canada, the United Kingdom and sanctioned countries, and say: "You must not use VPNs, proxies, or other tools to circumvent geographic or jurisdictional restrictions."
2. Open app.txflow.com , sign in and enter the code.
3. Deposit USDC. The docs say: "Deposits below 5 USDC are not supported on our platform and cannot be credited."
4. Trade perpetual futures, and keep some trading going every week if you want points. Warning At high leverage a small move can liquidate you. The docs say that in a full liquidation "all of the margin backing that position is consumed", and none of it comes back.
5. Check your points dashboard each Tuesday.

Withdrawals cost 1 USDC through the official Arbitrum bridge.
