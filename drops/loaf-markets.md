## Steps

1. Open beta.loafmarkets.com/league and press Join Competition.
2. Sign up. The docs say you can "register with email or connect a Web3 wallet". Warning This is a testnet. The app warns: "Do NOT send any real funds to your address as you won't be able to recover them".
3. Link X if you want to use referrals. It's optional.
4. Wait in the queue. The app shows your estimated round and says it will notify you when you have a seat.
5. When you have a seat, trade during the round with the mock funds you're given. Place at least one trade, or you won't get a rank.
6. If you finish in the paid places, use Claim Prize. "Winning players have 30 days from the conclusion of a round to nominate a valid payout destination". After that the prize is lost, so Round 3 winners have until about 5 December.
7. Follow @loafmarkets for Round 4 dates.
