## Steps

1. Open the Orbinum Hub and verify your wallet, Discord, Telegram and X.
2. Claim 5 test ORB a day from the faucet . It checks that you're in the Orbinum Discord.
3. In Private Pool, unlock your vault by signing a free off-chain message.
4. Shield at least 1 ORB, send a private transfer, unshield, and share a disclosure key, pasting each hash or key into its quest. Each quest can be claimed three times a week, once a day. Warning Each hash or disclosure key can be claimed once, by one member. Keep yours private until you have claimed it.
5. Do the weekly social quests and keep your streak going.
