## What it is

ChimpX, formerly Chimp Exchange, opened its $CHIMP airdrop checker on 28 October 2026. Claims unlock a month after launch. Who qualifies and the risks.

Nothing stated in the source.

## Steps

1. Open token.chimpx.ai and scroll to the airdrop checker.
2. Paste your Solana or BNB Chain address and press Check. "You'll never be asked to sign or send anything here." A wallet outside the list shows: "This wallet isn't in the snapshot."
3. If your allocation is on a BNB Chain wallet, you'll need a Solana wallet too: "How to link it is announced before claims open."
4. Wait for the claim, about a month after launch, and follow @ChimpxAI for the date. Warning ChimpX says: "We will never DM you first. Never share your seed phrase or private keys." Anyone offering an early claim or a sale spot by message is a scammer.

## Who is behind it

ChimpX's blog names "Akshay Nassa, CEO &amp; Co-Founder", and Dhruval Gupta wrote on X that he has "been building this quietly as CTO at @ChimpxAI." In May 2026 ChimpX announced a $2.8 million seed round: "The round was co-led by Waterdrip Capital and MetaLabs Ventures, with participation from Mindfulness Capital, NEURALHASH Capital, Marshland Capital, Maven Capital, Attention Venture, and BRINC."

## Costs

Checking costs nothing and needs no wallet connection. ChimpX hasn't said what claiming will cost; on Solana it should be a small network fee. Earning more HOOTs was never free in practice: the app's free plan charges a "1.0% flat swap fee", monthly plans cost $9 or $19, and a SuperChimp NFT that doubled HOOTs sold for 0.20 SOL.

## Risks

ChimpX publishes no terms of service, no company name and no list of excluded countries. Bots and multi-wallets are cut: "No appeals.No exceptions." $CHIMP was sold several times before this launch. In February 2026 ChimpX said it "Raised $100K on @Spores_Network" and promised a "Listing Price: $0.40" on PancakeSwap that never happened. A further public sale was set for 12 October on Metaplex.
