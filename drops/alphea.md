## What it is

## Steps

1. Install ALPHEA Connect from Google Play . It's Android only, and you sign up with email, Apple or Google.
2. Open the app each day, keep it on screen for your sessions and claim the daily quest rewards before midnight UTC.
3. When a round opens, tap Redeem in the app. It sends you to hub.alphea.ai, where you should "Log in with the same account as ALPHEA Connect."
4. Choose how many points to redeem and submit. They're gone once accepted: "the Points used for that Redeem are deducted, and the deducted Points cannot be used again."
5. When the round settles, claim your APT on the Hub within 30 days. Warning Only link a wallet on hub.alphea.ai. ALPHEA says it "does not request or store a User's private key, Seed Phrase, recovery phrase, or Wallet password through Connect", so anyone asking for those isn't ALPHEA.
