## What it is

River pays points each season that convert into Staked RIVER. Season 7 runs to 31 Dec 2026, when unclaimed Season 6 points are lost. Steps, costs and risks.

## Steps

1. Check that you're allowed. River's terms say: "You do not reside in the United States, Canada, or other excluded jurisdictions" and ban VPNs used to get around that. You must be 18 or older.
2. Open app.riverai.inc/airdrop . Don't use any river.inc link.
3. Connect your wallet and your X account. If you earned Season 6 points, "The page displays the eligible River Pts balance". Claim them before 31 December 2026.
4. To convert, check the live rate and the daily cap first, then "Choose your Staking Epoch, 3 to 24 months" and confirm on BNB Chain. Warning Staked RIVER stays locked until the date you pick, and a short lock gives up most of the amount. The price of RIVER can fall a long way while it's locked.
5. Check the leaderboard after each Saturday snapshot. The Season 7 claim date isn't set: River says it will be "confirmed through official announcement".

## Risks

River has rewritten the rules more than once. In November 2025 it paused conversions and bought back points it had sold, then launched new conversion systems in December 2025 and May 2026. What you get depends on what everyone else does. River's own example: "Same input, same 3-month staking epoch, three different rates, three different amounts of Staked RIVER."
