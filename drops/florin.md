## Steps

1. Open the campaign page on Superboard and sign in. We did not go past the sign-in button, so we can't say what the account asks for.
2. Do the two X tasks: follow Florin, then engage with its Testnet V2 post from the link on the page.
3. Get test ETH for gas. Florin's faucet links to the Robinhood Chain faucet for it. That page showed us a bot check, so we could not read its limits.
4. During market hours, borrow at florin.so/open: deposit SPY, set the FUSD amount and pick a rate. The smallest loan is 1,000 FUSD, and the app notes: "Your wallet needs 0.001 ETH for liquidator compensation, plus ETH for network fees." That is test ETH.
5. Deposit some FUSD in the Stability Pool at florin.so/stability.
6. During market hours, open a Multiply position at florin.so/multiply. "The loop must borrow at least 1,000 FUSD." Then verify each task on Superboard and repeat the last four until each counter shows 3/3.
