## Steps

1. Check you're eligible. Ondo Perps and Ondo Points exclude the US, Panama and other prohibited jurisdictions.
2. Sign in at app.ondoperps.xyz with email or a wallet such as MetaMask, Phantom or Coinbase Wallet.
3. Send USDC on Ethereum or Arbitrum, or eligible tokenized stocks on Ethereum, to your account's deposit address.
4. Trade perps. Your balance appears on the points page after each weekly drop.

## Who is behind it
