## Steps

1. Open dapp.4dlabs.space/tasks directly. Warning We found no task that asks for a seed phrase. Anyone who asks for yours is running a scam.
2. Log in with "a crypto wallet, a traditional email address, or a social media account such as Google or X". Check-ins need a connected EVM wallet, so use a spare one instead of your main wallet.
3. Put a little BNB in that wallet on BNB Chain. Each check-in is a transaction, and you pay the gas.
4. Pick a continent and press Check In each day. The docs recommend "checking in on the continent where you are located", but all six pay the same.
5. Join the Discord server and the Telegram channel from the Social section. That's 100 Score without touching your X account.
6. Check your Score, rank and linked accounts on the Profile page, and follow t.me/official_4dlabs for news of later seasons.
