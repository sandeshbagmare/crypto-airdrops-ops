## Steps

1. Open daejin.xyz/loyalty , the Jin Points page. The address daejin.xyz/points from Daejin's post of 17 November returned an error when we tried it on 20 November 2026.
2. Unlock your points. The Season 0 post puts it as "Register and link X or Discord to unlock your Jin Points". Locked points do not count at a snapshot.
3. Do the free tasks each day: the daily check-in, then "Take a side in Market of the Day, or take your free pick".
4. Optional: once you reach a badge, make a Jin Pass card from your profile and post it on X for the 24-hour boost.
5. Check your rank before each Thursday snapshot at 00:00 UTC.
