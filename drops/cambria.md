## Steps

1. Check that real-money gaming is legal where you live. We couldn't read Cambria's terms (see below).
2. Create an account in the Cambria lobby (referral link) with a wallet, email or social login.
3. Buy keys with USDC and play Dungeons runs to earn Royal Favor. Warning This is wagering with real money. Once keys are risked on a run, you can get back less than you put in.
4. Watch Cambria's blog and X account for the token and Genesis dates.

## Costs

Earning points means risking money. A key costs $0.10 in USDC, and a run can currently risk up to 50 keys, about $5. Unrisked keys can be swapped back for USDC at face value. Cambria's Dungeons economics docs page on 18 October 2026, with the flow from a USDC deposit to keys, runs, Dungeon Coins and a weekly redemption. Source: Cambria docs

## Risks

This is wagering. BlockchainGamerBiz reported that 87% of the $41 million wagered in the Genesis event went through Dungeon Fishing, a mode it calls gambling-heavy.
