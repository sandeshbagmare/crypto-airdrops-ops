## Steps

1. Check where you live. Cancore says "trading, swaps and liquidity provision are not offered to users located in the European Union or the European Economic Area." If you live there, you can't earn swap points.
2. Pick "Continue with Cancore Wallet" or "Continue with Loop Wallet". The Cancore Wallet is "Encrypted in your browser, 12-word backup". Write the 12 words on paper, because "A user who loses both their device and their recovery phrase loses access to their funds, full stop."
3. Fund the wallet. The welcome screen offers "Receive CC, USDC or cBTC". Keep some CC spare for Canton network fees.
4. For swaps to or from an EVM chain, link MetaMask: "Navigate to Profile → Wallets → Link EVM Wallet."
5. Swap on cancore.io. Every swap counts toward Genesis Points, and each one pays 0.5% of the amount you send plus network fees.
6. Join the Cancore Discord for the points rules, and follow t.me/cancore_io for news of a snapshot.
