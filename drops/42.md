## Steps

1. Check the restricted list below, then open www.42.space . The docs are at docs.42.space. Ignore other domains.
2. Sign in with an email address or an existing wallet, and use the same method each time. A referral code is optional: you can choose "or, skip to enter app".
3. Fund your account with USDT: "make sure to only send USDT on BSC to the wallet address." Or use the swap option, where "All tokens will be swapped and bridged to USDT on Binance Smart Chain (BSC)."
4. After each Wednesday close, check the Points page. Claim winning positions from your portfolio once a market resolves.
