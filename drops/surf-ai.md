## Steps

1. Open asksurf.ai and sign up. Surf's chief executive has warned about impersonators: "The only official link is https://asksurf.ai. The account is @SurfAIHQ."
2. Use the free plan first to see whether the tool is worth paying for. It includes limited research and three deep research sessions a week.
3. If you buy a plan, Waves come with it, and higher plans earn more.
4. Track your Waves on the leaderboard page, and share your referral link if you want the referral reward.
