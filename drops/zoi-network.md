## Steps

1. Install the Zoi app from the App Store or Google Play . The seller in both stores is Zoi Labs LLC. Warning Zoi's site also offers a direct Android APK "for people who cannot use Google Play". Use the store versions. A fake APK passed around in chats is an easy way to lose a phone's data or a wallet.
2. Sign in with Google, with Apple on an iPhone, or with a code sent to your email. If a friend gave you an invite code, enter it for the one-time 20 ZOI. You don't need anyone's code to join.
3. Do the face check when the app asks. "No ID document is needed to sync. Sync only asks for a quick face check that runs on your phone, to confirm a real person is behind the account."
4. Tap Sync each time the four-hour window opens, up to six times a day, and at least once a day to build the streak.
5. Open the Welcome chest in the app and finish its tasks within 7 days for 100 ZOI.
6. Later, sending ZOI will need a one-time check "with an ID document and a face check, by a verification provider". Zoi targets that for the first half of 2027 and says it will be free.

## Costs

Joining costs nothing: "It is free to join and sync: no hardware, no deposit." The app carries ads from "Google AdMob, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Pangle, Unity Ads", and Zoi says "Ads are never required to earn rewards." Once transfers open, "Every transfer pays a fee of 0.5 ZOI". The real cost is your time, a few taps a day, and the device and ad data the app collects.
