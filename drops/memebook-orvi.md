## Steps

1. Install a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare on your Android phone.
2. Download the ORVI app from Google Play. Warning The "Mint Your NFT" link on the old memebook.app site leads to an address that no longer exists. Don't mint from lookalike sites that claim to replace it.
3. Sign in with your wallet. This is a message signature that costs no gas, and ORVI's terms say: "We never ask for, receive or store your private key or seed phrase."
4. Finish the short entry quest to get the free tier.
5. Post, comment and check in daily to build XP and a streak.
6. Open the claim page on orvi.fun with the same wallet to see whether an early user reward is recorded for you.
