## Steps

1. Get an invite code. RISEx is in gated mainnet, and codes come from existing traders, the team or the waitlist referral leaderboard.
2. Go to rise.trade , click Trade Now and sign in with Google or an EVM wallet.
3. Enter the code, enable trading and switch to the RISE network.
4. Deposit USDC from your wallet or to a deposit address.
5. Trade. Your balance updates after each weekly drop on the points page .
