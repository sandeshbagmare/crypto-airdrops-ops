## Steps

1. Use only backtrade.xyz , its app, docs and partners subdomains, and @backtrade_xyz on X.
2. Press Join waitlist and enter a wallet address and an email. The form can answer "Not available in your region." and no country list is published. Warning The form takes your email, but the docs say: "The terms, privacy policy and risk disclosure are being prepared with counsel and are not published yet."
3. Deposit at least 10 USDC, counted after fees, from Hyperliquid or HyperEVM. A first deposit to an unused address also pays a one-time 1 USDC activation.
4. Pick BTC or ETH, long or short, from $10 of margin. Warning At 1000x on BTC, "a move of about 0.052% against you liquidates the position, and the whole margin is lost".
5. Losses mint BACK by themselves. Claim its USDC in the app, and withdraw your free balance at any time (at least 10 USDC through the app).
