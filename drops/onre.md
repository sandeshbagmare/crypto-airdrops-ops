## Steps

1. Check the excluded countries list first. The app blocks restricted locations, and Romania, the UK and the US are on the list.
2. Use a Solana wallet: "Any Solana wallet supported by WalletConnect (80+ compatible wallets)." The Open Access route needs no sign-up and no KYC.
3. Get ONyc. On app.onre.finance/earn , choose Open Access, enter USDC or USDG and press Purchase. "ONyc currently mints against USDC and USDG." You can also buy it: "Secondary liquidity is available 24/7 via Orca, Raydium, Jupiter."
4. Hold it for 1x, or move it to a supported venue for 2x to 6x, plus the Kamino 10x boost while it lasts. Warning Looping borrows against your ONyc, so a price drop can liquidate the position, and liquidity pools carry impermanent loss. Use leverage only with money you can afford to lose.
5. If you want referral points, make a code in the dashboard. A wallet you refer only counts after it holds 100 ONyc.
6. Track your total on the leaderboard or the public points API: "The ONyc Points API is publicly accessible and does not require an API key."
7. Plan your exit before you buy. Open Access holders sell on a DEX; redeeming through OnRe needs a verified wallet and, per OnRe's own disclaimer, qualified investor status.
