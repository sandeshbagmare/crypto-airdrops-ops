## Steps

1. Check that you can use it. The app says "Our services are not available to the United States, Hong Kong and other restricted jurisdictions". The terms ban getting around this "via VPN or proxy".
2. Open the official app at app.stablestock.finance , or the mobile app linked from stablestock.finance, and create an account. Warning Type the address yourself. StableStock never needs your seed phrase, and a site or a Telegram message asking for one is a scam.
3. Deposit USDT or USDC. The app text says "A minimum deposit of just 1 USDT." Only put in money you could afford to lose in the stock market.
4. Buy or hold stocks and ETFs that carry the Points badge. Spot trades count, and so do leveraged spot trades, which need KYC first.
5. Open the Points dashboard the next day after 02:00 UTC to see your points. If you used StableStock before 2 November 2026, look for your Retroactive Points and the OG Boost there.
6. Optional: once you have $3,000 of your own trading volume, create a referral link under Referrals.
