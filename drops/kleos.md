## Steps

1. Bridge HYPE to Elysium with the in-app Bridge. The Fast bridge, "through Relay, moves HYPE and tokens between Elysium and HyperEVM or other chains in seconds, for Relay's fee plus 0.5%." Keep some HYPE for gas.
2. Trade coins on Kleos. Your daily points follow the HYPE you buy and sell, and coins launched on Kleos count double.
3. Join a clan, or create one, on the Clans page. "Creating, joining and leaving are a signed message from your wallet, not a transaction." A clan holds 25 wallets, and once you join, "You can leave whenever you like, but you can only join another clan after that." 7 days later. Most top clans are already full.
4. After your first trade, check in each day on the Rewards page. "Free: you sign a message, no transaction."
5. Optional: from 15 November, prove your Hyperliquid HYPE stake with the passport on the Portfolio page for the boost. It takes one HyperEVM transaction.
