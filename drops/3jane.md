## What it is

3Jane pays locked JANE tokens every week to USD3 and sUSD3 depositors instead of an airdrop. How the farms work, the 1,000 USDC minimum and credit risks.

## Official site

https://app.3jane.xyz/supply

## What it needs

Nothing stated in the source.

## Steps

1. Get USDC on Ethereum, plus ETH for gas.
2. At app.3jane.xyz/supply , connect your wallet and deposit at least 1,000 USDC to receive USD3.
3. Optionally stake USD3 for sUSD3, which is locked for a month. Warning If a borrower defaults, sUSD3 absorbs the loss before USD3 does. Leaving sUSD3 takes a month's lock, a cooldown and a withdrawal window.
4. Check and claim your JANE each week on the farm page .

## Who is behind it

Paradigm led a $5.2 million seed round in 3Jane in June 2025, The Block reported. The website is run by Tulkum Assets Corp., a Panamanian company, according to its terms of use. ## Costs

The minimum deposit is 1,000 USDC, plus Ethereum gas. The FAQ says USD3 redemptions carry no fee. The farm page's rewards APY rests on a JANE valuation you can set, $250 million when we checked, so it is an estimate.

## Risks

USD3 redeems only up to the tranche ratio. JANE stays locked until the final mint, and its value depends on a supply and valuation nobody knows yet. 3Jane lists audits by Veridise, Sherlock, Electisec and Guardian, but smart contract risk remains. Credit lines are only for U.S.-based borrowers, and the website terms warn that jurisdictional blocks may stop you from using the site.
