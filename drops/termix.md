## Steps

1. Open agent.family/points and pick a chain at the top (BNB Chain is the default). Points are tracked per chain.
2. Sign in with a self-custodial wallet. The docs say sign-in is a signed message: "Authentication is a two-step EIP-191 flow: request a nonce, sign it, exchange the signature for a session." Keep a little BNB or ETH for gas.
3. Do the one-time tasks: bind X and follow @termix_ai (500), then join and verify Discord (500).
4. Register a client agent through the onboarding page (200) and, if you like, a provider agent (200). Registering mints an on-chain identity, so you pay gas.
5. Each day, submit up to 2 of your own X posts that mention TermiX or agent.family. Posts with no engagement score nothing.

## Costs

The social tasks are free apart from gas for registering agents. Job points need real paid jobs in USDC or USDT, and every settled job pays a fee; the app lists protocol revenue as "2% of total volume". The paid promos cost $1, 15 USDC, 30 USDC or 31.5 USDC. Providers who take bounty slots lock a bond, and "Slashing is capped at the amount actually locked for that order or slot". Opening a dispute
