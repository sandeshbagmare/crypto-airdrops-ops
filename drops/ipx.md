## Steps

1. Open ipx.exchange/fanszn , the link on IPX's official X profile.
2. Press Sign in with X. The rules ask for an "X account at least 90 days old with 50+ followers (or manual review)".
3. Pick a fandom and take the Fan Check. You get one try, so answer fast.
4. Come back every day to keep your streak multiplier, and do the side tasks: Discord, Telegram and the waitlist.
5. Post your own content about IPX, tag @IPXExchange and submit the link for scoring. Paid posts don't count: "Anyone paid by IPX or its agencies for campaign posts is not eligible for rewards."
6. Check the leaderboard before 15 November at 23:59 UTC and the results on 17 November. IPX hasn't said how winners link a wallet or claim. Warning Ignore anyone who messages you about prizes. The rules say: "IPX never DMs first. Only trust ipx.exchange and x.com/IPXExchange."
