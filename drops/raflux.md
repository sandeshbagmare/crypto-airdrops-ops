## Steps

1. Open raflux.io/quests and sign in with Google or a wallet. "Google sign-in: Privy creates an embedded Solana wallet for your account. No wallet extension is required."
2. Do the one-off quests: connect X, connect Telegram and verify your email.
3. Check in every day to keep the streak, and do the X tasks while they're up.
4. Share your referral link from the Refer and Earn page if you want. "Self-referral is not permitted."

## Costs

Signing up and the quests are free. Pulls cost 10 to 100 USDC each plus network fees. "Raflux does not add a platform fee to Super Gacha pulls, buyback, or physical redemption." Selling an item back loses at least 10%, and "Collectors pay shipping for domestic and international delivery." In the raffle marketplace, "an entry does not guarantee an item."
