## Steps

1. Start from fhenix.io and use only the links on it, including @fhenix on X. The games run on outside hosts such as vercel.app and onrender.com, so open them from Fhenix's own pages.
2. For credits, open onlyfhens.fhenix.io, connect a wallet and an X account, and do the three required quests. The post and video quests unlock after that.
3. For shop credits, play one of the listed games with one wallet, then sign in to the shop with that same wallet.
4. After 21 October, watch @fhenix for the first mainnet apps. Fhenix hasn't published any steps for users yet.
