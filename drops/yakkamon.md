## Steps

1. Pre-register with an email address at yakkamon.com . No wallet or Sunflower Land account is needed.
2. Tap your egg daily to build a streak.
3. Link Discord and X to your trainer profile and do the weekly quests.
4. Connect a wallet before the leaderboard is finalised on 27 November (UTC), since the NFT airdrop needs one. Rankings are hidden from 00:00 UTC on 26 November, so check the important dates page for any change.
