## Steps

1. Check that you can win. The contest is "open to individuals aged 18 or older, with a valid email address", and residents of about 80 countries are excluded (the list is below).
2. Open play.coinzilla.com and press Enter. Move with the arrow keys or A and D, and pause with the space bar. On a phone, the game asks you to turn it sideways.
3. Play a game past 30 points, then sign in with your email address and the code it sends. Use an inbox you read, because that is how winners are told.
4. Keep playing until 18 November at 23:59 EET. Only your best saved game counts, and "Each person may take part with one account only."
