## Steps

1. Open beta.pursuit.games , which pursuit.games links to, and join the waitlist with your email. You can add your X handle.
2. Share your friend link if you want to move up the line. "Codes go out in waves."
3. Wait for your code by email, or reply to Pursuit's 5 November post on X , which invites people to ask for a code there. Some codes are also posted by @pursuitsportsgg.
4. Sign in with a passkey, a wallet, X or email, pick a manager name and enter your code. A wallet is created for you.
