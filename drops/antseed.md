## What it is

Antseed pays a weekly ANTS airdrop to people who spend USDC on AI models through its network. ANTS can't be transferred or sold yet, and entry costs money.

## Official site

https://antseed.com

## What it needs

Nothing stated in the source.

## Steps

1. Add funds. You can pay by card where it works or send USDC on Base. "Each card payment is between $1 and $200, with at most two decimals."
2. Set your rewards wallet. "Your ANTS go to the authorized wallet set in the AI VPN or console, under Rewards."
3. Use paid models from a provider with a boost above 0x. Compare boosts on antseedpoints.com .
4. After Thursday at 09:54 UTC, claim your ANTS from the points site with your rewards wallet, or stake it straight into a provider's pool.

## Who is behind it
