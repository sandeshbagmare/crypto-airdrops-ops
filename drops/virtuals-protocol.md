## What it is

The Virtuals Protocol veVIRTUAL staker airdrops are live as of 27 October 2026. When the airdrop module is active for a launch, up to 5% of that agent token's supply goes to VIRTUAL stakers. The Virtuals app dashboard lists several such airdrops dated 25 to 27 October 2026. Taking part means staking

## Steps

1. 1 Step 1 : Open the Virtuals dashboard Go to the Virtuals dashboard , where your veVIRTUAL, locks and airdrops are shown.
2. 2 Step 2 : Connect a wallet that holds VIRTUAL on Base Staking is currently only supported on Base, so the VIRTUAL you stake needs to be on that network.
3. 3 Step 3 : Stake VIRTUAL to receive veVIRTUAL Use the Stake button on the dashboard. Locking for longer earns more veVIRTUAL.
4. 4 Step 4 : Check the airdrops list The dashboard lists each agent airdrop with its date and your allocation, for example Degen and Siren on 27 October 2026.
