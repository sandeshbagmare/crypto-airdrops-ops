## Steps

1. Check your region. Rewards is "currently unavailable in the United Kingdom", and Perps excludes more places (listed below).
2. Install MetaMask only through metamask.com , then open the mobile app. Warning Fake wallet apps exist to steal seed phrases. Don't follow install links from social posts or ads.
3. Open the Rewards tab and opt in. MetaMask says: "You will need to opt-in to the MetaMask Rewards program, and then opt-in to individual campaigns."
4. Open the Perps Competition and opt in to it as well. Read its rules there, since "The rules you find about a campaign in MetaMask Mobile are the official source of truth."
5. Fund perps and trade through MetaMask until 12 November, reaching the $10K minimum. In the June round, "If you trade directly on Hyperliquid or through another interface, those trades won't count." Warning Leverage can wipe out your deposit. Don't trade more than you can lose for a shot at a contest prize.
6. Check the leaderboard on the campaign page. In June, "Unrealized PnL from open trades will not count toward the leaderboard", so a winning position had to be closed to count. Assume the same until the rules say otherwise.
7. Look at the Benefits tab for one-time offers. "You will need to connect MetaMask to the partner's website to claim the benefit", so use only the links inside the app.
