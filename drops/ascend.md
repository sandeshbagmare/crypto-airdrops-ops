## Steps

1. Follow @AscendLaunch on X. Mint dates and allowlists will be posted there.
2. Enter the whitelist giveaways Ascend runs on X. One post offered five whitelist spots to the community. Builders can also be nominated, and Ascend says it will pick some nominations for NFT whitelist spots.
3. If you already hold HYPE, KNTQ or a Hypurr NFT, you may fall in an allocation group. Ascend hasn't said how holdings will be checked.
4. When the mint opens, mint on Ascend's official app only, then stake the NFT and wait the three-day activation.
5. Once a points season starts, read its terms before trading or staking for points. Warning Coins on Ascend are new memecoins with fees of up to 10% per trade. Ascend said copies of other teams' coins appeared during testing. Don't trade just to farm points you can't value.
