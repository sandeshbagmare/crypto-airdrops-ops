## Steps

1. 1 Step 1 : Connect a wallet on the Quests page Open the Katana quests page and connect a wallet to see your XP, rank and daily rate for the October 2026 season. The official rules are linked there.
2. 2 Step 2 : Deposit into an earn vault Pick a vault on the Earn page , such as the USDC vault . Each vault lists its own XP rate.
3. 3 Step 3 : Trade perps or borrow for extra XP Perps trades earn about 1.5 XP per dollar on the perps markets , and trading from the United States is blocked. Borrowing, for example USDC on the USDC market , earns up to 1.5 XP per dollar daily.
4. 4 Step 4 : Check your XP and open Krates XP is credited daily at 00:00 UTC. Once you hold 350K XP, you can spend it on a Krate from the Krates tab , or keep it as an entry toward the season prizes.
5. 5 Step 5 : Opt in to the perps competition If you also trade perps, opt in on the competition page before the round ends on 11 November 2026.
