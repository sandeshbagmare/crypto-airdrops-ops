## What it is

Ryfinite has no classic airdrop: quests earn Gems, and Gems committed to its pool earn RFN allocations. No token is out yet and the team is anonymous.

## Steps

1. Go to ryfinite.com and create an account: "You create a Ryfinite account with an email and password, or by signing in with Google."
2. Link your X account. The app says: "Link your X account once. It's used for every X quest and for joining the Launchpool." Link a wallet only if a quest asks for it, and use a fresh one. Warning Ryfinite's terms say: "We never ask for your seed phrase and will never request it." Anyone who asks is a scammer.
3. Do quests and keep your daily streak to collect Gems.
4. Open the RFN Launchpool, check the pool figures and "Choose how many Gems you want to commit." Assume those Gems won't come back: official posts say "Burn your Gems", while another page describes locking them. No official page says whether a position can be exited.
5. Check that your allocation shows on your account. Nothing arrives in your wallet until around TGE, and only after the anti-sybil review.
6. Optional: join the Qern campaign for XP before it ends.
