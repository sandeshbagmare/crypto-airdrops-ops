## What it is

Monolith's swanUSD shares 100,000 SWAN among snapshot-listed addresses that hold 1,000 sSwanUSD for 7 days by 19 November. Newcomers cannot qualify.

## Official site

https://www.swanusd.com/airdrop

## What it needs

Nothing stated in the source.

## Steps

1. Open swanusd.com/airdrop from a link you typed yourself and enter your Ethereum address in the checker. If it is not listed, it cannot qualify for the launch pool.
2. Stake a little over 1,000 swanUSD to get at least 1,000 sSwanUSD.
3. Optionally deposit the sSwanUSD in SWAN-rewards staking. It still counts for the airdrop and also earns SWAN emissions.
4. Keep at least 1,000 sSwanUSD for 7 full days. Start no later than 12 November 2026 at 16:00 UTC.
5. Watch @MonolithMarket for the claim date, then claim before the expiry. Monolith says it never sends DMs first, so ignore anyone who messages you with a claim link.

## Costs

There is no entry fee, but you need about $1,000 or more in sSwanUSD locked up for at least 7 days, and every step pays Ethereum mainnet gas: minting or swapping, staking, depositing and claiming. If you mint, paid debt carries a variable interest rate and free debt is 0% but can be redeemed against your collateral. Liquidations carry a penalty of up to 10%.
