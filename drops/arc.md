## Steps

1. Join Arc House and finish the onboarding profile for your first 50 points.
2. Spend your time where the points are: events, useful forum answers and original articles.
3. If you build, get free testnet USDC from Circle's faucet and deploy on the Arc testnet.
4. Add Arc mainnet to your wallet and try its apps. Gas is paid in USDC, so a small balance is enough. Warning Mainnet apps use real funds. They are built by other teams, and each one carries its own smart contract risk.

## Who is behind it

Arc is built by Circle Internet Group, a publicly traded company and the issuer of USDC.
