## Steps

1. Open di.xyz/check-in and check the address bar. Use a wallet that holds only a little BNB.
2. Press Connect &amp; sign in and choose your wallet. "First login requires a free signature. No gas or asset transfer."
3. Switch the wallet to BNB Chain and keep some BNB in it. Without it the page answers: "Add enough BNB to cover network gas, then try again."
4. Come back on 30 days in total. A wallet that starts on 18 November 2026 reaches 30 on 17 December at the earliest.
5. For extra points, link your X account in the Task Center ("Link an X account before starting X tasks."), follow DI, join the Telegram group and do the daily tasks that are open. If the X link fails, the page's advice is "Avoid in-app browsers and VPNs."
6. Keep an eye on DI's X account for the Genesis NFT mint. Its date, price and rules aren't published.
