## Steps

1. Open rarelist.io and join with X. The sign-in asks for "tweet.read users.read offline.access", so it can read your account but can't post for you.
2. Pass the account check: "Rarelist checks your account age, smart score and followers." The limits aren't published, and small accounts get this message: "Your account is too small to participate on Rarelist. Grow it to start earning."
3. Post the verification text, or your own version: "Post the text below, or write your own with @rarelistio and your verification code." Then wait: "We review your account and your post. Activation usually happens within 24 hours."
4. Do the join tasks on the launch post. For the reply, the rule is: "Write a reply in your own words. Copied one-line replies may be rejected during review."
5. Pick a live campaign under Projects and finish its required tasks: "Complete all mandatory tasks to be eligible for campaign rewards."
6. Post your own content about the project on X.
7. Paste your Ethereum, Solana and Robinhood Chain addresses into your profile. Plain addresses only: "ENS names are not accepted. Paste the 0x address." Warning Rarelist asks only for X sign-in and public addresses. If anyone asks for a seed phrase, a private key or a wallet signature in its name, treat it as a scam.
